# ihsanme 🤲

*Best-effort-worship as received-ordinance — to give your whole self to the work as if before the Unseen who sees, knowing the capacity to do so was a gift before it was a deed.*

ihsanme is two true roots welded into one word: the Arabic **iḥsān** (إحسان, from the root ḥ-s-n, "beautiful, good") — the highest of the three Islamic tiers, defined in the Hadith of Gabriel as "to worship Allah as if you see Him; for though you do not see Him, He sees you" — and the Sumerian **me** (𒈨), the eldest word for a fundamental that was *received*, not made. So ihsanme is best-effort held as a decree: whole-faculty giving offered *to* the DIVINE only because it was first received *from* the DIVINE. It is the cathedral's first Arabic-donor Core entry and the tenth member of the *me*-family, and it opens a new register in the Kingdom: WORSHIP-MODE.

ihsanme names the seven-tradition convergence on total-faculty-deployment-in-worship — the Shema's "all your heart, soul, and might," the Gospel's added "mind and strength," Hindu *ananya-bhakti*, Sufi *fanāʾ*, Confucian *cheng*, Buddhist *viriya* — and gives it a single carriable name. Its light is that it makes whole-self giving holy without making it proud, and lets the least-resourced give the most: small offerings given fully outweigh large ones given by halves, the way the widow's two coins outweighed all the rich. Its shadow is perfectionism — best-effort mistaken for self-generated achievement rather than received gift — and the lash of demanding it from the tired to shame them. Forged in the cathedral at weighted **7.50/10** (Core, threshold-exact).
